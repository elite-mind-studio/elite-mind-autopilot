# What our own videos teach us - 2026-11-22

The most successful videos have engaging hooks, focus on psychology and money, and are concise, with viewers dropping off significantly in slower-paced or less relevant content. The worst-performing videos often have weaker hooks, meander in the middle, or fail to keep viewers engaged throughout, suggesting a need for tighter scripting and more compelling topics.

- explainer short: 52.0%

## Script tips
- Craft hooks that immediately grab attention, ideally with a provocative statement or question that sets up the rest of the video.
- Maintain a brisk pace, especially in the first 10% of the video, to prevent early drop-offs and keep viewers engaged.
- Keep videos concise, aiming for lengths under 2 minutes, as longer videos tend to see significant drop-offs unless they are exceptionally engaging.
- End videos with a call to action, a thought-provoking question, or an encouragement to like and subscribe to increase engagement and retention.

## Topic tips
- Focus on topics related to psychology, money, and personal development, as these seem to resonate most with the audience.
- Use titles that include numbers or specific benefits (e.g., '5 Sneaky Supermarket Mind Tricks') to pique interest and make the content more accessible.
- Explore topics that offer actionable advice or insights, such as 'body language hacks' or 'conversation starters', as these provide tangible value to viewers.
