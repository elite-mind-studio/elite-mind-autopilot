July 1983. A brand new Boeing 767 is cruising at forty one thousand feet over Canada. Sixty nine people on board.

Then, an alarm. One engine stops. Then the other.

The plane has run out of fuel.

How? Canada was switching to the metric system. The fuel was worked out with the wrong number, pounds instead of kilograms. They had taken on less than a quarter of the fuel they needed.

Now the plane is a glider. With no engines.

But here is the incredible part. Captain Bob Pearson was also an experienced glider pilot.

His first officer remembered an old air force base nearby, at Gimli. Pearson glided the jet down and landed it there.

Every single person survived.
