Remember the narrow strip of sea that controls the world's oil? It just got more dangerous.

On the fourth of November, Iran's Revolutionary Guard said four oil tankers struck naval mines south of the Strait of Hormuz, and were still burning.

It said the tankers were using a route Iran had declared prohibited, and that two more ships were stopped. These claims have not been independently confirmed.

Mines are what ships fear most. They are hard to find, hard to clear, and they can hit any vessel that passes. Every new one pushes insurance costs higher.

The strait has been mostly shut since March. Iran says it will reopen it, but only if Washington accepts its demands. So far, there is no deal.
