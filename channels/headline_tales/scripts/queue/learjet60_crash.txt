This private jet fell about five miles in one minute. And investigators may have found a clue to why.

On Sunday, a Learjet 60 was flying from Austin, Texas, to the Bahamas, at thirty-five thousand feet.

The crew reported ice and turbulence, and asked to climb higher.

Then it dropped from about thirty-five thousand feet to under nine thousand in a single minute.

That is a dive of more than thirty thousand feet per minute, almost straight down onto a Florida road.

The NTSB says there is evidence that some parts may have broken off the plane during that dive. All nine people on board were killed.
