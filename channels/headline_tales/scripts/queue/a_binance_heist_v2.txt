In just twenty minutes, hackers took six point three billion dollars from the world's biggest crypto exchange.

Last week, attackers ordered Binance's own wallets to send their coins to wallets the hackers controlled, across ten different blockchains.

It is the biggest crypto heist in history, more than four times the previous record.

Reports say it began with one hacked laptop, belonging to a Binance engineer.

The FBI blames a North Korean hacking unit known as TraderTraitor. North Korea routinely denies these attacks.

About three billion dollars has been frozen. But more than three billion is still out there.

Binance says the losses will not be passed on to its users. Still, Bitcoin fell to a six-week low.
