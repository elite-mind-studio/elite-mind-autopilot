You probably know the story. A teenage conman pretends to be a Pan Am pilot, a doctor and a lawyer, and cashes millions in fake checks. It became the movie Catch Me If You Can.

Frank Abagnale told that story for decades. In books. In speeches. On TV.

But here is the twist. Most of it was not true.

In 2020, a writer named Alan Logan dug through old prison and court records. He found that during much of the time Abagnale said he was flying around the world as a fake pilot, he was actually in a prison in New York.

Pan Am never employed him. The hospital said the doctor shift he described never existed. And his federal case was about ten forged checks, worth less than fifteen hundred dollars.

So the greatest con of Frank Abagnale's life may have been the story itself.
