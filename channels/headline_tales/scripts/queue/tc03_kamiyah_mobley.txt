Have you ever heard of a girl who couldn't get a job, because she had been kidnapped as a baby? Sit tight. Let's go on a journey.

Jacksonville, Florida, the tenth of July, 1998. A sixteen year old mother, Shanara Mobley, gives birth to a baby girl. Her name is Kamiyah.

Just eight hours later, a woman in a blue flowered smock and green scrub trousers walks into the room. She looks like a nurse. She picks up the baby, and she walks out of the hospital.

Kamiyah is gone. Her family searches for eighteen years.

In Walterboro, South Carolina, a girl grows up as Alexis Manigo. She is loved. She finishes high school. The woman she calls mum is Gloria Williams.

But in 2015, Alexis wants a job. For that, she needs her birth certificate. And her mother can't give her one.

But here is the part nobody expected.

The questions lead Alexis to the truth, and to a family she has never met. Investigators are alerted. In January 2017, a DNA sample taken at her birth confirms it. Alexis is Kamiyah Mobley.

Gloria Williams pleaded guilty, and in June 2018 she was sentenced to eighteen years in prison.

And the most surprising part? Kamiyah still calls her "my mother." She even wrote a letter asking the court to shorten her sentence. The court said no.

One baby. Two mothers. Which one would you choose?
