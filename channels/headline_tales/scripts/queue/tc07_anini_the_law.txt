Have you ever heard of the armed robber who made a whole Nigerian city afraid, while a police officer was secretly helping him? Sit tight. Let's go on a journey.

Benin City, Nigeria, the nineteen eighties. A young man named Lawrence Anini leads a gang of armed robbers. People give him a name that says everything. The Law.

In early 1986, two of his gang members are tried and jailed. Anini believed some officers had promised to make the evidence disappear. He felt betrayed. And he turned on the police.

Between August and October 1986, his gang was blamed for killing nine police officers.

The city lived in fear. Rumours spread that he had powerful charms, that he could vanish. Newspapers across the country called it the Anini saga.

But here is the part that shocked Nigeria.

Anini had friends inside the police. The biggest was a police inspector named George Iyamu, accused of supplying the gang with guns and information.

Nigeria's military leader, Ibrahim Babangida, demanded action. On the third of December 1986, police found Anini at a house in Benin City. He was shot in the leg, and arrested.

At the trial, Iyamu denied ever knowing him. Anini shouted back, "You are a shameless liar!"

On the twenty ninth of March 1987, Anini, members of his gang, and police officers convicted with them were executed by firing squad in Benin City.

Almost forty years later, people in Benin still tell the story of The Law.
