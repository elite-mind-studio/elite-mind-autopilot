One man has put four hundred and forty-six million dollars into America's midterm elections.

That man is Elon Musk, the world's richest person. According to Federal Election Commission data, he is the biggest individual donor of this election cycle.

The money went to super PACs. These groups can raise and spend unlimited amounts, as long as they do not coordinate directly with candidates.

About two hundred and sixteen million went to America PAC, the group Musk set up in twenty twenty-four. Another one hundred and sixty-eight million went to the main super PAC backing Republican Senate candidates.

The next biggest donors? Richard and Elizabeth Uihlein, with about one hundred and fifty million to Republican-aligned groups, and George Soros, with about one hundred and two million to Democratic-aligned groups.

And total political ad spending this year is projected to reach eleven point six billion dollars, the most in American history.
