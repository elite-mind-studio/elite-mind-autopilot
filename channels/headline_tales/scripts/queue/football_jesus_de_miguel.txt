A routine chase for the ball ended a young man's life.

On Monday, deep in stoppage time, Tenerife striker Jesus de Miguel, thirty years old, ran onto a long ball. Andorra's goalkeeper, Owono, rushed out to clear it, and his knee struck de Miguel in the chest.

He suffered a cardiac arrest on the way to hospital. On Tuesday morning, his club announced that he had died.

But this is also a story about the man on the other side. According to La Vanguardia, the goalkeeper, Owono, was seen crying and praying in the dressing room. He has since blocked comments on his social media, after a flood of insults.

Police have opened an investigation. But the chief prosecutor said criminal charges are unlikely, because it happened during play.

UEFA said it searched for words, and found only silence.
