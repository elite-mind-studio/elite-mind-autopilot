Iran shut a strip of sea just thirty-nine kilometres wide, and the whole world started paying for it.

Ships that tried to cross were attacked, so the biggest shipping companies stopped sailing.

That trapped one in every five barrels of the world's oil, which sent oil prices soaring to around one hundred and eighteen dollars a barrel.

The Philippines declared a national energy emergency. Twenty thousand sailors were stuck at sea for months.

And on October twenty-fifth, only two ships made it through.
