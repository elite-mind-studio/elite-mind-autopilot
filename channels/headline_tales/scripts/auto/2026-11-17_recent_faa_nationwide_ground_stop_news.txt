I bet you didn't know this. Nearly eight thousand flights were cancelled and more than ten thousand delayed when a nationwide ground stop hit the United States. The four-and-a-half hour halt began just after 3:30 p.m. Friday, grounding departures while roughly three thousand five hundred aircraft stayed aloft. Investigators say a newly installed router began sending a thousand connection attempts every few seconds, flooding the FAA's network and crippling communications at seventeen control centers. A White House "tiger team" of FAA, intelligence, defense and transportation officials met within hours to prevent another outage.
