I almost didn't post this. A visitor tossed a knit hat, a ceiling fan flung it into Fiona's enclosure, and the hippo died.

The hat slipped through a small opening above the viewing windows, and Fiona swallowed it about ten days before her death.

Zoo veterinarians sedated her, performed a colonoscopy with a nine-foot scope, and even borrowed a vibration plate from a horse farm to shake the obstruction through.

Ultrasound showed intestinal bunching and the hat moving through her small intestine, but surgery isn't an option for full-grown hippos.

Despite offering her favorite foods, coffee scent, and constant monitoring, the synthetic hat swelled, blocked her intestines and proved too large to pass.
