Three suspects used AI-generated voice and video to pose as corporate CEOs and big-law attorneys, and they convinced employees to wire over one hundred twenty-nine million dollars to foreign accounts. 
The scheme targeted accounting departments at hospitals in Missouri and California, a bank in Rhode Island, a fintech firm in the United Kingdom, a mutual fund in Massachusetts and a pet-food company in Florida, among others. 
During calls the scammers mimicked the executives' speech and even showed fabricated video, then directed victims to a fake attorney who instructed a wire transfer. 
After the money moved, the trio converted the funds into cryptocurrency to hide the source before splitting the proceeds. 
Federal investigators say the operation ran from July two-thousand twenty-four to February two-thousand twenty-six,
