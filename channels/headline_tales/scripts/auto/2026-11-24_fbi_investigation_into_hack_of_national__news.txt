I almost didn't post this. The FBI discovered an apparent hack of its National Crime Information Center, the central database that police use for fugitives, missing persons and stolen property. The scope remains unclear, and the bureau has declined to comment while the hack was first reported by CNN and confirmed to the Associated Press. President Trump called the news "not good" in a Fox interview, highlighting political concern. Historically, the FBI has been a frequent target, with recent intrusions into its job portal and surveillance systems.
