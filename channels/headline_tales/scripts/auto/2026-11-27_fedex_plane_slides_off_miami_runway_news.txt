You won't believe this. A FedEx MD-11 cargo plane overshot the runway at Miami International Airport Wednesday night, forcing all but one runway to shut down just before Thanksgiving.
The aircraft, arriving from Memphis, ran off the runway at about eight forty p.m., but officials reported no injuries.
With three of the airport's runways converging at the west end, the incident halted most of the airport's operations, leaving only a single runway usable.
The Federal Aviation Administration launched an investigation and logged hours-long flight delays for passengers heading into the holiday weekend.
Miami International urged travelers with Wednesday night departures to check with their airlines for updates and thanked them for their patience as crews worked to resume normal service.
