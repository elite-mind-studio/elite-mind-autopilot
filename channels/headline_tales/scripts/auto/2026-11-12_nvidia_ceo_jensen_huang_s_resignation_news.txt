I have a question for you. Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's chief executive today, and the board said it was after an investigation into an alleged affair with a woman U.S. agencies suspect was spying for China.

The news sent Nvidia's stock down more than eight percent in after-hours trading, pushing the share price to $159.51.

That drop continued a market-value decline of over $450 billion since the probe was announced earlier this week.

Investors fear the loss of the company's long-time visionary, with analysts saying there is no clear successor to fill his shoes.

Interim chief financial officer Colette Kress will run the company while a permanent CEO is searched for, and the board says operations and product roadmaps will continue uninterrupted.
