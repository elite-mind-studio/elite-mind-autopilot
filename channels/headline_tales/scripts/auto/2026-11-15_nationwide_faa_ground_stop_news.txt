I bet you didn't know this. A nationwide ground stop forced the FAA to halt all flights on Friday, stranding thousands of travelers across the country.

The outage stemmed from overloaded routers during a telecom upgrade meant to replace aging copper lines with fiber in remote facilities.

Officials said L3Harris Technologies was performing the maintenance when the new router began flooding other routers, crippling the network.

Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy blamed the contractor, called for a $50 billion congressional appropriation, and said the old network must be replaced.

He noted that over seventy percent of copper lines have already switched to fiber without incident, but the incident shows the urgency of a full rebuild.
