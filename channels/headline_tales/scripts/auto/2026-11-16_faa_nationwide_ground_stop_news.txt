You won't believe this. Nearly eight thousand flights were canceled when the FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop on November thirteenth.

The four-and-a-half-hour halt forced departures from every US airport to pause, while airborne planes were allowed to finish their routes.

More than ten thousand other flights were delayed, leaving gates packed, passengers stranded, and even Amtrak's phone lines flooded as travelers searched for alternatives.

Sports teams, wrestling events, and even trans-Atlantic flights had to scramble for new plans, with some chartered buses and later flights taking the place of the missed planes.

Investigators say a newly installed optical-fiber router was sending a thousand connection attempts every few seconds, disrupting aircraft communications, but the exact cause is still being examined.
