The breach was first reported by CNN and confirmed by an unnamed source to the Associated Press, prompting the bureau to launch an investigation on Saturday.
The NCIC, maintained by the FBI's CJIS division, connects federal, state, tribal and local law-enforcement systems, making any compromise a potential threat to everyday safety.
Officials have not disclosed who accessed the system or how extensive the exposure is, and the FBI declined to comment as the probe continues.
This incident follows recent cyber-alerts at the USDA and multiple government outages, intensifying worries about the nation's overall cyber defenses.
