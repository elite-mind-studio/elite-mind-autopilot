You won't believe this. The FBI is investigating an apparent hack of its National Crime Information Center database, the core system law-enforcement agencies use to share warrants and missing-person alerts.

The breach was first reported by CNN and confirmed to the Associated Press by an anonymous source, but 

President Donald Trump said in a Fox News interview that he had just heard about the incident and called it "not good," while the FBI declined to comment.

Cyber-security experts note the FBI has been a frequent target, recalling previous attacks on a job portal and internal surveillance systems, underscoring the rising threat to government networks.
