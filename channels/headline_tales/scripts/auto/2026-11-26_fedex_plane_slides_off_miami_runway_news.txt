You won't believe this. A FedEx cargo plane overran a runway at Miami International Airport Wednesday night, forcing all but one runway to close just before Thanksgiving.
The McDonnell Douglas MD-11 was coming from Memphis when it encountered an issue on landing, and the aircraft slid off the pavement at about eight forty p.m.
Miami-Dade fire units responded, but officials reported no injuries among crew or passengers.
The Federal Aviation Administration opened an investigation, and airlines warned passengers to check for updates as hours-long delays built up.
By early Thursday, the airport was working to resume normal operations, urging travelers to stay patient.
