I almost didn't post this. A FedEx cargo plane slid off a Miami International Airport runway Wednesday night, forcing all but one runway to shut down.
The McDonnell Douglas MD-11 was coming from Memphis and, according to officials, experienced an issue on landing; fortunately, no injuries were reported.

The Federal Aviation Administration opened an investigation and warned travelers of hours-long delays as crews worked to clear the aircraft.
With Thanksgiving travel looming, the disruption threatened to affect thousands of holiday plans, prompting the airport to urge passengers to check with their airlines for the latest updates.
