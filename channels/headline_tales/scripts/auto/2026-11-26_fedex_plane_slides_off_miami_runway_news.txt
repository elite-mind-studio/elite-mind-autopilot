I almost didn't post this. A FedEx cargo plane slid off a Miami runway on Wednesday night, shutting down all but one runway on the eve of Thanksgiving.
The MD-11, coming from Memphis, ran onto the grass at about 8:40 p.m., and no injuries were reported, according to airport officials.

Miami International handles over a thousand daily flights and is a top-five U.S. hub for Thanksgiving, with the American Automobile Association expecting eighty-one point five million Americans to travel this week.
Passengers were urged to check with airlines for updates, while FedEx coordinated with investigators and confirmed its crew was safe.
