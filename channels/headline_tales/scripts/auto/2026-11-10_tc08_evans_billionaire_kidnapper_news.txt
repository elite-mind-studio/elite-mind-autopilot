Have you ever heard of a kidnapper so rich that Nigerians called him the billionaire kidnapper? Sit tight. Let's go on a journey.

His name is Chukwudumeme Onwuamadike. Most people know him as Evans. He comes from Nnewi, in Anambra State.

For years, police in Lagos, Edo and Anambra had him on their most wanted lists. Police described him as smart, crafty and intelligent. For four years, he kept slipping away.

His targets were wealthy business people. According to police, some of his ransoms reached up to a million dollars.

February 2017, Lagos. Donatus Dunu, the managing director of Maydon Pharmaceuticals, is kidnapped. The kidnappers demand five hundred million naira. According to the court, one hundred and fifty million naira was paid.

Meanwhile, Evans was living the high life, with property in Lagos, and in Accra, Ghana.

But here is the part nobody expected.

On the tenth of June 2017, officers from the police Intelligence Response Team walked into his mansion in Magodo, one of the richest areas of Lagos. They arrested him in his own bedroom. Videos of police officers celebrating went viral across Nigeria.

In August 2017, Evans and fifteen others were charged with fifty two counts.

In February 2022, Justice Hakeem Oshodi of the Lagos High Court found him guilty of kidnapping Donatus Dunu, and sentenced him to life in prison. The judge said he showed no remorse.

He later received another twenty one year sentence for a different kidnapping, and he still faces more charges.
