I bet you didn't know this. The FBI is investigating an apparent hack of its National Crime Information Center database, the system that shares fugitive, missing-person and criminal-history data with agencies across the country.

The scope of the intrusion is still unclear, and the FBI has not commented publicly on who may be responsible.

Officials note the agency has long been a target for hackers, citing prior attacks on its job portal and internal surveillance systems.

The incident arrives amid a string of recent government cyber-outages, though there is no indication it is linked to those events.
