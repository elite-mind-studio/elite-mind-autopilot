I almost didn't post this. The FBI discovered an apparent hack of its National Crime Information Center, the database that shares criminal records with federal, state and local agencies. 

The breach was first reported by CNN and confirmed by an AP source, but the bureau declined to comment on the scope of the intrusion. 

Officials say the cause and extent of the breach remain unknown, and there is no immediate indication it links to recent nationwide tech outages. 

President Trump, speaking on Fox News, said he had just heard about the hack and called it "not good," adding he hopes it might reveal new information. 

Cyber-security analysts warn that attacks on critical law-enforcement systems are rising, even as the FBI's own experts are regarded as globally respected.
