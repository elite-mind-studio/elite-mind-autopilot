Interim CEO Colette Kress said the firm has stopped shipping its Hopper AI chips to China, cutting a $2.4 billion revenue stream that made up about two percent of total sales.
The move follows revelations that Huang had a relationship with a woman linked to Chinese intelligence, raising national-security concerns.
Despite the scandal, Nvidia reported fiscal third-quarter earnings of $65.3 billion, more than double a year ago, and revenue of $115.8 billion.
The board also created a panel to oversee national-security policy and export-control compliance, signaling a tighter regulatory stance.
