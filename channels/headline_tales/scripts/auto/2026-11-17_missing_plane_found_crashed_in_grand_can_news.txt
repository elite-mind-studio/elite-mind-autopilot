You won't believe this one. A small Piper Comanche that vanished near the remote North Rim was found crashed in the Basin area, and there were no survivors.
The three people on board remain unnamed as officials withhold their identities to protect grieving families.
The aircraft was reported missing around eleven thirty p.m. Sunday, prompting a multi-agency search that began in the early hours of Monday.
Rescue teams used infrared and night-vision gear, and the wreckage was located at about ten fifteen a.m. on Monday.
Investigators from the National Transportation Safety Board will now lead a probe, assisted by the Federal Aviation Administration, to determine what caused the accident.
