I almost didn't post this. An eleven-month-old infant was discovered clinging to life after being buried in a dog crate for about twenty-four hours.
The child, Koah Sharp, vanished Monday when his father, Michael Comer, took him from his grandmother's home while she ran an errand.
Nearly one hundred officers scoured the woods near the grandfather's house, spotted freshly disturbed soil, and began digging.
As they removed earth they heard the baby cry, pulled the towel-wrapped crate out, and rushed him to a hospital.
Comer was arrested that night on burglary, attempted murder and other charges, and is being held without bond.
Sharp was released from the hospital Tuesday night, expected to make a full recovery and now stays with family members.
