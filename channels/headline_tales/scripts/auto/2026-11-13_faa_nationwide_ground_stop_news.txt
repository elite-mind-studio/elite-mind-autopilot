You won't believe this one. A rare nationwide ground stop halted every departing flight in the United States today.
The Federal Aviation Administration said a sudden telecommunication outage affected all Air Route Traffic Control Centers, forcing the action for safety.
Secretary of Transportation Sean Duffy and Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin both said there was no indication of a nefarious act, only a communications failure.
Flight tracking showed roughly three thousand one hundred aircraft were airborne when the stop began, and within hours over ten thousand delays and two thousand cancellations were logged.
Investigators from the FAA and the Transportation Security Administration are now probing the radio-frequency issue that silenced pilot communications.
