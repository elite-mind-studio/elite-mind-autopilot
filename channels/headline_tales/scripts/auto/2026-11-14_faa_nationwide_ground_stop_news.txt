You won't believe this. A rare nationwide ground stop grounded every departing U.S. flight Friday, and stranding thousands of travelers.
The FAA ordered the stoppage at 3:36 p.m. EST while about 3,500 planes were in the air, and all airborne aircraft landed safely.
A preliminary investigation points to an overload of aging Cisco 600 series routers built between 2001 and 2006, causing a telecommunications outage and inconsistent data flow.
The halt lasted about five hours, ending around 8:20 p.m. EST, and airlines are now scrambling to reschedule flights and reposition crews.
Tens of thousands remain stuck in airports, many unsure where they'll sleep tonight, while officials assure the sky is safe to fly again.
