You won't believe this one. 
The outage stemmed from Cisco 600 series routers, built between two thousand one and two thousand six, that became overwhelmed and fed out-of-sync data into the air traffic control system.
Because the routers flooded the network with inconsistent packets, voice lines went static and screens blacked out, forcing the FAA to shut down the system for safety.
The freeze lasted about five hours, leaving more than two thousand delays and nearly four thousand cancellations, with Delta, United and American hit hardest.
