He was a respected Wall Street legend. Bernie Madoff. Former chairman of the Nasdaq stock market.

For decades, rich families, charities and ordinary savers trusted him with their money. His returns were steady. Never too high. Almost never a bad month.

That was the trick. Because there were no real investments. New investors' money was used to pay the old ones.

By 2008, his clients' statements added up to about sixty five billion dollars. But much of that money had never existed.

Then the financial crisis hit, and people wanted their money back. There was not enough.

But here is the twist. In December 2008, Madoff told his own sons the truth. It was all one big lie.

His sons went straight to the authorities.

He was sentenced to one hundred and fifty years in prison.
