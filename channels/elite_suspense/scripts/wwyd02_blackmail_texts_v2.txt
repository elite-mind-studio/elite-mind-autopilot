A stranger texts you.

I have photos you really don't want anyone to see.

Eight hundred dollars tonight. Or I send them to your friends, your family, and the people you work with.

Your hands are shaking. Your heart is pounding.

You feel completely alone.

[pause]

The most dangerous part of blackmail isn't always the secret they know.

It's the fear they put inside your head.

They want you isolated.

They want you ashamed.

They want you to believe that paying them is the only way to save your life.

[pause]

But listen carefully. You are not powerless.

Do not pay. Paying almost never makes it stop.

Stop replying. Save the evidence. Every message. Every username.

Secure your accounts.

Tell someone you trust, and report it to the FBI at tips dot F B I dot gov.

If you are under eighteen, use Take It Down to stop the images spreading.

Because a secret does not give another person ownership of your life.

And the moment you stop facing the threat alone, is the moment they begin losing control.
