A stranger texts you.

I have photos you really don't want anyone to see.

Eight hundred dollars tonight. Or I send them to your friends, your family, and the people you work with.

Your heart is pounding. And that is exactly what they want.

But look closer. They just put the amount, the threat, and their account in writing. That is evidence.

So here is what you do.

Do not pay. Paying tells them you are scared, and they almost always come back for more.

Do not reply. Not even to say no. Silence tells them you are not playing.

Screenshot everything. The messages, the username, the payment details. Do not delete a thing.

Then block them, and report it. On the app, and to the FBI at tips dot F B I dot gov.

If you are under eighteen, tell an adult you trust, and use Take It Down to stop the images spreading.

They thought fear gave them control. All they did was hand you the evidence.
