Imagine your phone rings at eleven at night.

A voice says the richest man in the city wants to hire you. Tonight.

You take the elevator to the top floor.

The office is huge. And completely empty.

Every desk is spotless. Every chair is pushed in.

And every screen in the room shows the same thing. A clock. Counting down.

Fifteen minutes.

On the desk in front of you, there is a contract. And a gold pen. Already open.

The salary is one million dollars a year.

You read it twice. Everything looks normal.

Except one line, at the very bottom. Once you sign, you can never leave.

It has to be a joke.

Then the door at the end of the room opens.

He walks in slowly. Perfect suit. Calm smile.

He does not sit down. He just says, everyone who signed this is still here. And they have never been happier.

Ten minutes.

He slides the pen closer. Your name is already printed under the line.

Behind him, a cleaner is mopping the floor. She never looks up.

Five minutes.

He leans in. Sign, and you will never worry about money again.

The cleaner moves past your chair.

And so quietly you almost miss it, she whispers.

I signed it too. Twenty years ago.

One minute.

You have two choices.

Sign it, and take the million dollars.

Or walk away, and trust a woman with a mop you have never met.

So, what would you do?
