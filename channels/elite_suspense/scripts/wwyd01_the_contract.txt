Imagine your phone rings at exactly eleven forty seven at night.

The richest man in the city is calling.

He offers you one million dollars a year.

But you must come to his office. Tonight.

When you arrive, every computer screen shows the same countdown. Fifteen minutes.

On the desk, there is a contract. One million dollars every year. A luxury apartment. A private driver.

And one strange condition. Once you sign, you can never leave.

You laugh. It has to be a joke.

Then he turns his laptop towards you.

A live camera feed. Twenty three people, sitting in different offices.

Every person in that building signed this contract, he says. They are all rich. And none of them wants to leave.

Then you notice something.

Nobody in the camera feed is moving.

You ask him why. He just smiles. Because they understand what they have.

Five minutes.

The cleaner pushing a trolley stops beside your chair.

Without looking at the billionaire, he whispers. Don't sign it.

Why, you ask.

He slowly rolls up his sleeve. On his wrist, a tattoo. Employee number one.

I signed it twenty years ago.

Then why are you still cleaning this place?

He leans closer.

Because I tried to leave.

[pause]

Three minutes!

Your phone lights up. A message from an unknown number. Your predecessor signed. He is still here.

Thirty seconds.

The pen slides towards you. One million dollars. Every year. Just sign.

You have two choices.

Sign the contract. Or walk away.

But remember. The cleaner has been waiting twenty years for someone to make the right choice.

So, what would you do?
