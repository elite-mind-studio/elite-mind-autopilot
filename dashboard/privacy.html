Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how the Elite Mind Decoder automation ("the App") and this dashboard website ("the Site") handle information. The App is a private tool used only by the owner of the Elite Mind Decoder YouTube channel to create and publish videos to that channel.

1. Who uses the App

The App is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in to it with their own Google account. Visitors to the Site cannot sign in, upload, or change anything.

2. Information the App accesses

With the channel owner's permission (granted through Google's OAuth sign-in), the App uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and their titles, descriptions and tags to the owner's channel;
  • add those videos to the owner's playlists;
  • read the owner's channel and video statistics (subscribers, views, likes, comments) to show them on the Site.

The App only accesses the owner's own channel. It does not access other users' accounts, private messages, contacts or any other Google data.

3. How the information is used and stored

  • Sign-in credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored as encrypted secrets in the App's hosting environments (GitHub Actions and Vercel) and are used only to perform the actions above.
  • Public channel and video statistics are displayed on the Site and cached for up to one hour.
  • No information from Google APIs is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

4. Visitors to this Site

The Site does not use cookies, does not ask for personal information and does not run analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider (Vercel) may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability, under Vercel's Privacy Policy.

5. Google API Services and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

The channel owner can revoke the App's access at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party connections. Revoking access stops all uploads and statistics collection. Stored credentials are deleted from the hosting environments when the App is retired, and cached statistics expire automatically.

7. Children

The App and the Site are not directed at children under 13, and no information is knowingly collected from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Elite Mind Decoder YouTube channel's About page.
